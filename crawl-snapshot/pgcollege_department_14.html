Biographical Sketch

Dr. G.V.R. Sarma

Professor & Head, Chemical Research · Dr. Lankapalli Bullayya College, Visakhapatnam

Dr. G.V.R. Sarma is a distinguished chemist with over three decades of research, teaching, and industry experience spanning organic synthesis, medicinal chemistry, and natural products. He has made significant contributions to the discovery of novel molecules, several of which have advanced to clinical trials (Phase 2 and beyond). His broad scientific training — from the Indian Institute of Science (Ph.D.) to postdoctoral fellowships at leading institutions in India, Germany, and the United States — has equipped him with a unique multidisciplinary perspective.

Scientific Impact

Contribution to Science

Area 01
Organic Synthesis and Medicinal Chemistry
Dr. Sarma has synthesized and characterized more than 600 novel molecules over the course of his career, with several compounds advancing into clinical trials at Phase 2 and beyond. His work spans the design and synthesis of bioactive heterocyclic scaffolds, with applications in drug discovery and pharmaceutical development.
Area 02
Natural Products Chemistry
His research program encompasses the isolation, characterization, and total synthesis of natural products with therapeutic relevance. This work integrates classical synthetic methods with modern spectroscopic characterization techniques.
Area 03
Novel Materials
In addition to pharmaceutical applications, Dr. Sarma has contributed to the design and synthesis of novel functional materials, broadening the translational impact of his synthetic chemistry expertise.
Area 04
Research Mentorship and Capacity Building
Dr. Sarma has awarded 13 Ph.D.s with one candidate who has submitted. He mentored a DST-SERB National Post-Doctoral Fellow and served as Co-PI on a UGC-funded project. He was Principal Investigator on the DBT-SAHAJ institutional grant (₹20 Crores, provisionally accepted). MoUs established with Solara Pharma, Dr. Reddy's, and Pfizer for the B.Sc. Chemistry program reflect his bridge-building between academia and industry.
